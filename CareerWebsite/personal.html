Why I Chose Computer Science

I chose to research computer science because I enjoy working with technology and solving problems. I like learning how things work behind the scenes, whether it’s a website, a program, or a digital system. Computer science gives me a chance to be creative while also using logic and critical thinking. It’s a field that constantly changes, which means there is always something new to learn.
